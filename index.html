Privacy Policy

MoneyNest

Last updated: August 15, 2026

AFU Solutions ("we", "us", or "our") developed and operates MoneyNest ("the App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use MoneyNest.

Privacy at a glance: MoneyNest is designed as an offline-first personal expense tracking application. Your financial information is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a backend server or Firebase database for the App's core functionality.

1. Information We Collect

MoneyNest does not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal information to use its core features.

The App does not intentionally collect or transmit your financial information to AFU Solutions.

Information that you enter into the App may include:

  • Income amounts
  • Expense amounts
  • Transaction titles
  • Transaction categories
  • Transaction dates
  • Optional notes
  • Budget information
  • Currency preferences
  • App preferences
  • Theme preferences

2. How Your Financial Data Is Stored

Information entered into MoneyNest is stored locally on your device using local application storage.

The App's core functionality does not upload your transaction information to an AFU Solutions server.

Because your information is stored locally, AFU Solutions does not have access to the financial information stored inside your application.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or intentionally share your financial transaction information with third parties.

MoneyNest does not use Firebase or a remote backend database for storing your financial information.

4. Internet Access

The core MoneyNest expense tracking functionality is designed to work without an internet connection.

Certain functions, such as opening external links or using your device's email or sharing functionality, may rely on Android system services or other applications installed on your device.

5. Export and Import

MoneyNest may provide functionality to export your transaction information to a file, such as a CSV file.

When you choose to export or share a file, the file is handled according to the Android system and the application you choose to share it with.

You are responsible for choosing where you save or share exported financial information.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete your locally stored information using the application's data-management functionality.

MoneyNest may provide a Delete All Data option that allows you to remove locally stored transactions, categories, budgets, and related application information.

Uninstalling the App may also remove its locally stored application data, subject to the behavior of your Android device and any device backup services.

7. Data Retention

Because MoneyNest stores information locally, data generally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the application's data, uninstall the App, or otherwise remove the stored information.

AFU Solutions does not maintain a remote copy of your financial transactions through the App's core functionality.

8. Security

We design MoneyNest to minimize unnecessary collection and transmission of user information.

However, no electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should use appropriate security features provided by your device, such as a secure screen lock.

9. Children's Privacy

MoneyNest does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its core functionality.

10. Third-Party Services

The core version of MoneyNest does not use Firebase, a remote backend, or a cloud database to store user financial information.

If third-party services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be reviewed and updated as necessary to accurately describe the relevant data practices.

11. External Applications

Some functions, such as sharing an exported file or contacting AFU Solutions by email, may open applications provided by other companies on your device.

Those external applications operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, applicable requirements, or our practices.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Your Responsibility

You are responsible for ensuring that information you enter into MoneyNest is accurate and for maintaining backups of any important financial information you choose to export.

MoneyNest is a personal expense tracking tool and does not provide financial, investment, accounting, tax, or legal advice.
